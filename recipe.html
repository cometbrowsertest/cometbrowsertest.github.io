Guacamole, the Awesome Way

Creamy, bright, a little spicy—balanced for maximum scoopability.

Serves 4–6 • 10–15 min • Gluten-free • Vegan

Ingredients

  • 3 ripe Hass avocados
  • 1 small jalapeño or serrano, minced (seed for milder heat)
  • 1/4 cup finely diced red onion
  • 1 small ripe tomato, seeded & diced (optional)
  • 1/4 cup chopped fresh cilantro
  • 1–2 tbsp fresh lime juice, to taste
  • 1/2 tsp fine sea salt, plus more to taste
  • 1 small garlic clove, grated or mashed (optional)
  • Pinch ground cumin (optional, but excellent)
  • Finely grated lime zest from 1 lime
  • Finish: 1 tsp extra-virgin olive oil & flaky salt (optional)

Method

  1. Prep aromatics: In a bowl, combine onion, chili, lime juice, salt, cumin, and garlic. Let sit 5 minutes to mellow and bloom flavors.
  2. Add avocados: Halve, pit, and scoop in the avocados. Add lime zest. Mash with a fork or potato masher to your preferred texture.
  3. Fold-ins: Stir in cilantro and tomato (if using). Taste and adjust salt and lime. If it tastes flat, add a pinch of salt; if heavy, add a splash of lime.
  4. Finish: Optional drizzle of olive oil and a sprinkle of flaky salt. Serve immediately, or press plastic wrap directly on the surface and refrigerate up to 6 hours.

Pro Tips

  • Choose avocados: Gently press—slight give = ready. Check under stem nub: green is good; brown is overripe.
  • Onion choice: Red is sweet and vibrant; white is punchier and traditional. Rinse diced onion briefly to soften bite.
  • No watery guac: Seed and pat-dry tomato before adding.
  • Perfect texture: Mash 2 avocados smooth; dice the 3rd and fold in for chunkiness.
  • Heat control: Serrano = brighter heat. Remove membranes and seeds to dial it back.
  • Lift without souring: Lime zest boosts aroma so you can use less juice.
  • Keep it green: Press plastic directly on the surface, or float 1–2 tbsp cold water on top; pour off and stir before serving.
  • Serve with: Warm salted tortilla chips, tacos, or avocado toast with chili flakes.

Variations

  • Roasted poblano + corn: Fold in diced roasted poblano and charred corn kernels.
  • Chipotle–lime: 1–2 tsp minced chipotle in adobo + a touch of honey to balance.
  • Pico de gallo guac: Fold in well-drained pico for a chunky, herby style.
  • Pepita crunch: Top with toasted chopped pumpkin seeds and a drizzle of chili oil.
